共同规范：白底、二维平面、细线、无阴影与立体硬件图标。图内全部使用简短英文。建议深灰表示不变数据，蓝色表示当前有效数据，橙色表示修改或新写入，红色虚线表示失效或退休对象；颜色之外同时使用边框、箭头和文字区分含义，以适应灰度打印。CPU/GPU 用区域标签，不用处理器照片。

建议最终宽度约 175–180 mm，缩放后最小文字不低于约 8 pt。Figure 2 高约 65–80 mm，Figure 3 高约 40–55 mm，实际按版面调整。生成参考图可以采用较高分辨率，但最终论文优先人工重绘为 SVG/PDF，逐项校对标签、箭头与数学符号。AI 生成图不能作为系统实现的证据。

## 3. Figure 2(a)：源隔离与搬移范围

### 构图说明

子图内部左右对照，左为 `PMA: possible rebalance`，右为 `Source-isolated blocks`。两边各有 Before/After 两行，使用相同的插入请求 `Insert (u,d)`。

左侧画一段 PMA 区域，标出 u、v、w 的连续邻接与空隙。选择一个 u 附近空间不足、需要扩大重平衡范围的示例；After 行中 u、v、w 的起始位置都发生变化。用虚线框标出 `Rebalanced range`，用短箭头连接旧、新起点。强调这是一次可能发生的重平衡，不是 PMA 每次插入的必然行为。该图是概念示意，不需要伪造具体 PMA 密度阈值或地址数值。

右侧画 mapped host slab 内的三个独立块。u 原来为 `[a,b,c]`，容量为 3；插入 d 后换到更大块 `[a,b,c,d,空,空]`，旧块标为 `Retired`。v、w 在两行保持相同位置与内容，直接标 `Unchanged location`。画三个简化的 descriptor 行，只让 u 的位置箭头和 degree 从 3 更新为 4，v、w 的箭头保持不变。这是一次已选择为容量不足的示例，不能由图推断具体生产容量增长倍率。

子图结论只写：`Only u relocates`。CPU adjacency block 与 GPU cache slot 不出现在同一个抽象层中，避免混淆两种块。

### AI 提示词

```text
Create panel (a) of a scientific systems-paper figure, titled "Source isolation". Use a clean flat vector-like style on a white background, thin dark lines, readable English labels, no gradients, no shadows, and no decorative hardware icons.

Compare two storage layouts side by side for the same update: "Insert (u,d)". Within each layout show aligned "Before" and "After" rows.

LEFT: label "PMA: possible rebalance". Draw a schematic packed memory array containing contiguous adjacency lists for sources u, v, and w with gaps. Show one possible rebalance spanning all three lists after u grows. Draw a dashed bracket labeled "Rebalanced range" and arrows showing that the starting locations of u, v, and w can change. Do not imply that every PMA insertion moves every source. Do not include numerical density thresholds.

RIGHT: label "Source-isolated blocks". Draw independently allocated adjacency blocks within a region labeled "Mapped host memory". Source u initially contains [a,b,c] in a full block. After insertion it occupies a larger block containing [a,b,c,d] and spare slots. Mark u's old block "Retired" with a dashed outline. Keep v and w at exactly the same positions before and after, with labels "Unchanged location". Add a small descriptor table for u, v, and w; only u changes its location pointer and degree from 3 to 4. Label the result "Only u relocates". Block sizes are illustrative, not a specified allocation growth policy.

Use orange for changed data, muted blue for valid blocks, dark gray for unchanged structures, and dashed outlines for retired storage. Keep arrows short and noncrossing. This panel explains host adjacency allocation, not GPU cache allocation. Do not draw an immediate reuse of the retired block. Leave sufficient margins for inclusion as the left half of a two-panel publication figure.
```

## 4. Figure 2(b)：有效变更与多视图一致性

### 构图说明

以从左到右的三状态流程为主体：

```text
G_t: Adj(u)=[a,a,b]
  → Effective delete (u,a,-2)
G_t^-: Adj(u)=[b]
  → Recovery completes
  → Effective insert (u,c,+1)
G_{t+1}: Adj(u)=[b,c]
```

图顶部放一个小输入框：`Requests: delete (u,a) × 3; insert (u,c) × 1`。输入先连接到 `Group once by source`，再接两个 phase；插入请求可以提前分组，但插入动作必须在 recovery 之后。

在删除箭头下方显示 `1 unmatched request → no change`。每个有效变更分别分支到对应的 reverse 更新小框：`Overlay_a[u] += -2`、`Overlay_c[u] += +1`。这些写的是 overlay 的增量，不是完整 incoming 列表；不能画成删除 a 的所有入边。

底部显示 `S^-={u}` 和 `S^+={u}` 汇入 `S={u}`，再连接 `One final descriptor for u`。此箭头表示最终发布的数据来源，不表示这里已经进行了 GPU scatter。可加一条小字 `Same effective changes across views`。

这个例子用于展示重数与阶段语义，与 2(a) 的扩容例子独立。它的最终 degree 从 3 降到 2，不能画作触发扩容。

### AI 提示词

```text
Create panel (b) of a scientific systems-paper figure, titled "Shared effective changes". Match a clean flat vector-like style with a white background, thin outlines, muted blue valid states, orange updates, and gray unchanged data. All text is short readable English.

At the top, draw "Requests: delete (u,a) x3; insert (u,c) x1", leading to "Group once by source". Show the following main sequence from left to right:
1. "G_t" with "Adj(u) = [a,a,b]".
2. Arrow labeled "Effective delete (u,a,-2)".
3. "G_t^-" with "Adj(u) = [b]".
4. A mandatory box "Recovery completes".
5. Arrow labeled "Effective insert (u,c,+1)".
6. "G_{t+1}" with "Adj(u) = [b,c]".

Under the deletion arrow, show the annotation "1 unmatched request: no change". Branch the effective deletion to a small reverse-update box "Overlay_a[u] += -2". Branch the effective insertion to "Overlay_c[u] += +1". These are signed increments to reverse overlays, not full incoming lists.

At the bottom, show "S^- = {u}" and "S^+ = {u}" merging into "S = {u}", followed by "One final descriptor for u". This denotes the final publication set, not an intermediate GPU publication. Include the small explanatory label "Same effective changes across views".

Maintain a clear separation between update requests, effective changes, and graph states. Recovery MUST precede the insertion action. Do not cancel deletion and insertion into a single net update. Do not draw a GPU descriptor publication after deletion. Do not show this degree-decreasing example as a block expansion. Keep the diagram sparse and readable as the right half of a two-panel paper figure.
```

### 双子图拼版提示词

若分别生成 2(a)、2(b)，可在重绘时统一字体、线宽和色值。若一次生成，给出两份面板说明后追加：

```text
Combine the two specified panels into one wide academic figure. Place (a) on the left and (b) on the right with a clear gutter and equal visual weight. The two panels use independent examples: (a) demonstrates capacity growth, while (b) demonstrates multiplicity and phase semantics. Do not connect their adjacency arrays or imply that they describe the same batch. Use consistent fonts and a small shared legend. Keep the long figure caption outside the artwork.
```

建议图注已写入正文，最终制图时不必把整段图注嵌进图中。

## 5. Figure 3：两阶段维护、一次发布与安全消费

### 构图说明

采用三条横向泳道，时间向右：`CPU forward / reverse`、`GPU computation`、`GPU publication stream`。一条简洁时序比完整函数调用图更适合本章。

按顺序布置以下事件，位置可错开，但依赖箭头必须保持：

1. 左端公共边界 `Previous GPU work completes`。CPU 随后 `Reclaim eligible blocks`，准备本批分组。
2. GPU computation：`Invalidate on old state`。
3. CPU：`Prepare reverse^- → Apply deletes → Commit reverse^-`。用一个组合框容纳三个短步骤，并从 GPU invalidation 连入。
4. CPU：`Materialize incoming`，沿数据箭头传给 GPU `Recovery on G_t^-`。
5. 从 `Recovery completes` 回连 CPU `Prepare reverse^+ → Apply inserts → Commit reverse^+`，再到 `Build patches for S`。
6. GPU publication：`H2D patches → Scatter descriptors → Repair / invalidate cache → Record event E`。
7. GPU computation：`Wait E → Insertion propagation`。将 event 到 wait 的箭头加粗，它是 GPU 可见性的关键。
8. 最右侧下一批边界，用虚线箭头从退休块注记连接 `Readers complete → Eligible reuse`。不要让它看起来与本批 CPU mutation 并发。

顶部用淡色范围标注 $G_t$、$G_t^-$、$G_{t+1}$ 的语义阶段。这些是逻辑拓扑状态，不是三份同时保留的物理图副本。图上写 `One final descriptor publication`，只覆盖步骤 6。

为了可读性，容量预检可合并在 prepare 中，不必画 allocator、thread pool、radix passes 或所有 CUDA 同步调用。必须保留的关系是：reverse prepare 在 forward mutation 前，recovery 在 insertion 前，cache 一致性在 event 前，event 在依赖读之前，回收在读者完成之后。

### AI 提示词

```text
Draw a compact, wide, publication-quality timing diagram for an out-of-memory GPU streaming graph system. Use three horizontal swimlanes, top to bottom: "CPU forward / reverse", "GPU computation", and "GPU publication stream". Time flows left to right. White background, flat vector-like shapes, thin dark lines, generous spacing, short English labels, no 3D elements. This is a sequential batch execution with explicit dependencies, not an overlapping pipeline across batches.

Show this ordered sequence across the lanes:
- A shared starting boundary: "Previous GPU work completes"; then CPU "Reclaim eligible blocks".
- GPU computation: "Invalidate on old state".
- CPU composite block: "Prepare reverse^- / Apply deletes / Commit reverse^-", in that internal order.
- CPU "Materialize incoming", with a data arrow to GPU "Recovery on G_t^-".
- A dependency arrow from recovery completion to CPU composite block "Prepare reverse^+ / Apply inserts / Commit reverse^+", followed by "Build patches for S".
- GPU publication stream: "H2D patches" -> "Scatter descriptors" -> "Repair / invalidate cache" -> "Record event E".
- GPU computation: "Wait E" -> "Insertion propagation". Emphasize the event-to-wait dependency with one bold arrow.
- At the far right show a subsequent synchronized boundary "Readers complete", followed by "Eligible reuse". A dashed annotation connects retired host blocks to this later reuse, never directly to the mutation that retired them.

Use light background bands labeled "G_t", "G_t^-", and "G_{t+1}" to identify semantic stages, not simultaneous physical copies. Bracket the final H2D/scatter/cache/event sequence with "One final descriptor publication". Recovery reads materialized post-deletion incoming data; DO NOT draw an intermediate outgoing-descriptor publication. DO NOT let insertion precede recovery completion. DO NOT label a CPU epoch increment as GPU completion. DO NOT show cache repair after the publication event. Do not add an atomic transaction, per-access GPU version checks, or cross-batch CPU/GPU overlap.

Use orange for changed topology, blue for valid data and computation, and dark gray for control dependencies. Distinguish data arrows and synchronization arrows through solid versus dashed styles and a small legend. Optimize readability after reduction to a two-column paper width. Leave the long caption outside the image.
```

## 6. 制图后的内容核对

- 图 2(a) 左侧明确标注 possible rebalance；右侧只有 u 换块，v、w 位置保持不变。
- 图 2(b) 三次删除只产生 -2；恢复发生在插入前；最终集合只有一个 u。
- reverse 的 overlay 记录显示重数增量，不能误画成全量 reverse 重建。
- 图 3 只有一次最终 descriptor publication，incoming 传输与它分开。
- cache repair 表示完整源邻接的修补或失效，不画成只传新增边。
- retired 不等于立即 free；CPU epoch 不等于 GPU 完成。
- 若图中添加 `24|S| bytes`，须同时标为 `descriptor payload only`；不要将其标为 total PCIe traffic。
- 不添加“首次使用 Zero-Copy”、跨批双版本流水、任意权重平行边支持或未测量的加速数字。

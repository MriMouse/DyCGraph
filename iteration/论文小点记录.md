Incremental computation has been implemented in GPU systems such as Grapin by mapping its operators to GPU execution.[^gpu-incremental] Two limitations remain. First, assigning topology maintenance to the CPU and incremental computation to the GPU leaves CPU underused in preparing affected computation. We extend the CPU's role to extracting and organizing the dependencies of GPU-identified affected vertices in parallel, supplying compact inputs for GPU repair. Second, worklist-based processing that collects vertex states or rebuilds task lists across the graph to drive convergence incurs additional work outside the affected region. We use affected-vertex queues to guide CPU dependency preparation and GPU repair, then directly enqueue successful improvements for insertion propagation, avoiding full-graph state gathering and worklist reconstruction.
